About

Aaron Jones (b. 1993, Toronto, Canada) is a visual artist who is working across photography, collage, video, and installation. He has exhibited at museums, galleries, and institutions including but not limited to the McMichael Canadian Art Collection, Art Gallery of Ontario, Museum of Contemporary Art Toronto, Robert McLaughlin Gallery, McMaster Museum of Art, Fonderie Darling, and Mercer Union. His work is held in public, institutional, and corporate collections including the Art Gallery of Ontario, The Image Centre, Robert McLaughlin Gallery, McMaster Museum of Art, Massey College, Doris McCarthy Gallery at the University of Toronto, the Wedge Collection, Royal Bank of Canada, Equitable Bank, Scotiabank, and Pardon. Jones’s work was included in *Vitamin C+: Collage in Contemporary Art, published by Phaidon in 2023. In 2020, he received the Gattuso Prize for Best Featured Exhibition at the CONTACT Photography Festival. He is represented by Zalucky Contemporary.
